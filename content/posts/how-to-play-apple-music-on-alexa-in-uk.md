 You can now use Alexa to play Apple Music on Amazon Echo speakers.

 It’s only available for Echo devices and now customers in the UK can also connect their Apple Music account with Alexa.

  
 1. Open the Alexa app then sign into your Amazon account and make sure that your Echo device is connected.

 2. Open Menu (top left icon), then select settings.

 3. Select Music.

 4. Select "Link New Service"

 5. Tap the Apple Music icon

 6. You will be prompted to enable the Apple Music skill and asked to sign into Apple account and provide access.

 7. Finally choose Apple Music as the default music service.

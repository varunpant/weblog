Turn your iPhone or iPad into a powerful tracing tool. ArtTracer overlays any image onto your live camera feed, letting you trace directly onto paper, canvas, wood, walls, or any surface.

## How It Works

1. Choose an image from your Photo Library or Files
2. ArtTracer overlays it on your camera feed in real-time
3. Adjust opacity, scale, and position to fit perfectly
4. Trace onto your surface with confidence

No projector needed, no printing, no complicated setup.

## Two Tracing Modes

**Normal Mode** — Your image floats over the live camera feed. Pinch to resize, drag to reposition, rotate to angle it. Adjust opacity to see both the reference and your surface. Control the image or camera layer independently.

**AR Mode** — Place your image onto a real surface using augmented reality. Scale it, lock it in place, and trace with the image anchored in 3D space. Perfect for larger surfaces where the reference needs to stay fixed as you move.

## Built for iPhone & iPad

Fully optimized for every screen size. iPad gives you a larger working view, while iPhone keeps things portable. Adaptive layouts ensure everything looks right on every device.

Art Tracer turns your iPhone or iPad into a powerful tracing tool. It overlays any
image onto your live camera feed, letting you trace directly onto paper, canvas, wood,
walls, or any surface — with no projector, no printing, and no complicated setup.

Choose an image from your Photo Library or Files, line it up over your surface with
pinch, drag, rotate and opacity controls, and trace with confidence. Use **Camera mode**
to float the reference over the live feed, or **AR mode** to lock it onto a real surface
in 3D space for larger work like murals and walls.

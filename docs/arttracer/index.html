Two ways to trace

Two modes. Both remarkable.

Float your reference over the camera, or lock it into the real world with AR. Whatever you're making, there's a mode for it.

Camera mode.
Instant overlay.

Your image floats over the live camera feed. Pinch to resize, drag to reposition, rotate to angle it — and adjust opacity to see both the reference and your surface at once. Control the image and camera layers independently for a perfect line-up.

AR mode.
Locked to your world.

Place your image onto a real surface with augmented reality. Scale it, lock it in place, and trace with the reference anchored in 3D space — so it stays put as you move around. Ideal for large surfaces, murals and walls.
